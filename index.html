Welcome

Relaxed tropical luxury on the Andaman coast

Blue Orchid Beach is a small beachfront retreat where the day moves at the pace of the tide. Wake to sea-view balconies, drift between infinity pools and warm sand, and let golden hour fold slowly over the limestone karsts — the yellow orchid greeting every guest at the door.
