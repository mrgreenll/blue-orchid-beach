The Location

At the foot of the Krabi karsts

Blue Orchid Beach sits on the quiet shore of Ao Nam Mao, on Thailand’s Andaman coast — between calm water and dramatic limestone cliffs, minutes from the longtail piers to Railay and Ao Nang.

Getting here

Nearby

Longtail boats to Railay & Ao Nang; close to the Ao Nam Mao pier and Thara Park.
